# TopRC : Hornets Run

An RC-scale F/A-18 jet simulator that runs in the browser. Fly it like a real 1.25 m EDF model, or switch to the stabilised Arcade mode.

**Play:** https://tofulistic.github.io/hornets-run/

## Missions
- **Canyon run**: thread the gates down a frozen river gorge, stay under the radar ceiling, hit the bunker and climb out before 2:30.
- **Carrier ops**: bungee launch off a model carrier, fly a circuit, land and catch a wire.
- **Dogfight**: three bandits over the desert mesas. Lock, fire, flares.
- **Formation**: hold right echelon on the lead jet through a sunset circuit.
- **Free flight**: airfield, carrier, canyon or desert.

## Controls
- RadioMaster Pocket / any ELRS radio as a Bluetooth joystick, any gamepad, keyboard, or on-screen sticks.
- Use a phone as the controller: open **Controls → Show pairing code** and scan it with the phone. Nothing to install.
- Keys: W/S throttle, A/D rudder, arrows pitch and roll, Space fire, F flares, G gear, H hook, C camera, V cinematic view, T RC/Arcade, R reset, Esc pause.
