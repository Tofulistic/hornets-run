Fly it like a real RC jet
  1. Take-off: full throttle, keep it straight with rudder, pull back gently at about 12 m/s.
  2. Speed is life. In RC mode it stalls below ~14 m/s and a hard pull drops a wing. Push forward and add power to recover.
  3. Landing: line up long, throttle to about 30%, hold the nose a touch high, let it settle. Under 3 m/s sink or it breaks.
  4. Carrier: full throttle fires the bungee. Come back slow and steady; the hook catches a wire if you touch down on the back third.
Connect the Pocket over Bluetooth
  1. On the Pocket, use a model with the internal module set to CRSF / ExpressLRS.
  2. Press SYS, open ExpressLRS, choose [BLE Joystick].
  3. On the computer, pair “ExpressLRS Joystick” in Bluetooth settings.
  4. Come back, move a stick, open Controls and choose Set up controls.
